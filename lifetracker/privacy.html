隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 11 日
适用于 LifeTracker+（「LifeTracker+ 生活追踪」）iPhone App 和本网站

LifeTracker+ 没有服务器，开发者不收集你的任何个人信息。你记下的内容存在你的 iPhone 上；开启家庭共享后，也存在房主的 iCloud 里，只有房主邀请的家人能访问。查价格和汇率时，App 会把证券代码和货币对发给 Yahoo Finance，对方会看到你的 IP 地址。

开发者不收集数据

  • 没有账号，不用注册或登录。
  • 我们没有服务器。你记下的账户、金额、家务和其他内容，都不会传给我们。开启家庭共享后，这些内容存在 iCloud 里，由 Apple 保管，我们看不到。
  • App 里没有统计、广告、崩溃上报或追踪类的第三方代码，也不跨 App 或网站追踪你。
  • 我们不出售、不共享任何个人信息，手里也没有。

App Store 上这款 App 的隐私标签是「未收集数据」。家庭共享的数据存在房主的 iCloud 里，只有家里的人能读写，开发者和第三方都访问不到，所以不算收集的数据。

App 什么时候联网

App 没有自己的服务器，只在下面几种情况连接 Yahoo Finance：

  • 你在「资产」页点刷新时，查询持仓价格和用到的汇率。
  • 打开 App 时，如果汇率已有 2 天以上没更新、又有其他币种的记录，自动刷新一次汇率。
  • 你第一次用某个币种时，查询它的汇率。
  • 你在「设置 › 汇率」里，或者点缺少汇率的提示打开的汇率页里，换基准币种或点「刷新汇率」时。

发出去的只有证券代码（例如 AAPL、0700.HK）和货币对（例如美元兑新加坡元），不包括金额、数量、账户名称、成员或任何设备标识。和访问任何网站一样，Yahoo 会看到你的 IP 地址和请求的基本信息，比如时间。

Yahoo Finance 由第三方运营，按它自己的隐私政策处理请求，我们拿不到它的记录。价格和汇率随时可以手动填写，不联网也能用其余全部功能。App 还会连接 Apple 的 iCloud 和推送通知服务，见家庭共享。

另外，你在「设置 › 关于」里点隐私政策或支持时，链接在 Safari 里打开。

家庭共享

  • 家庭共享默认关闭。你在「设置 › 家庭共享」点「开启家庭共享」，或者加入家人的邀请、点「连接 iCloud 上的家庭」之前，App 不会往 iCloud 上传任何数据。这些都需要登录 iCloud。
  • 开启后，上面表格里的记录，以及基准币种、汇率、净资产目标和家庭所在时区，会上传到房主的 iCloud，占用房主的 iCloud 空间。模块开关、私密模式、涨跌颜色、金额按千取整和「我是」只存在各自的设备上。
  • 只有房主邀请的人能加入，App 不提供任何人都能加入的公开链接。加入的人都能查看、添加、修改和删除家庭里的任何记录。家人读写的是房主 iCloud 里的这份数据，App 不在家人的 iCloud 里另存一份。
  • 家庭里的每个人能在「家庭共享」里看到其他成员和已邀请但还没加入的人的 iCloud 名字；没有名字时，显示对方的 iCloud 邮箱或电话。
  • 数据由 Apple 按 iCloud 的规则传输和保存，App 没有另外加密。我们没有服务器，看不到这些数据。
  • 从 1.1.0 起，App 每次启动都会向 Apple 推送通知服务注册，没开启家庭共享时也一样，不会请求通知权限。家人改了数据时，iCloud 用静默推送通知 App 同步，不会弹出提醒。App 不会把推送标识发给我们或任何第三方。
  • 没开启家庭共享时，你打开「设置」，App 会向 iCloud 查询账号状态，以及 iCloud 里有没有你以前建的或加入的家庭，只读不写。

存在哪里，保留多久

  • 数据存在 App 在 iPhone 上的私有存储空间里，其他 App 读不到，并受 iOS 数据保护。
  • 数据会一直留在你的设备上，直到你删除它；开启家庭共享后，家人删掉的记录也会从你的设备上删掉。
  • 没开启家庭共享时，LifeTracker+ 不把数据存到任何云端。开启后，家庭数据同时存在房主的 iCloud 里，直到房主关闭家庭共享。卸载 App 不会删除 iCloud 上的家庭数据。
  • 如果你打开了 iPhone 的 iCloud 云备份，或者用电脑备份 iPhone，App 的数据会随设备备份，按 Apple 的规则保存在你自己的 Apple 账户或电脑里。我们访问不到这些备份。

备份、导出和导入

  • 在「设置」里可以把全部数据导出成一个 JSON 备份文件，包括已关闭模块里的数据。文件不加密，由你自己保管；用隔空投送等方式发给谁，由你自己决定。
  • 导入备份会替换这台设备上的全部记录。导入前会显示文件名、导出时间和记录数，你确认后才执行；中途出错时，原有数据不变。
  • 开着家庭共享时，只有房主能从备份恢复。恢复的内容会同步给全家，家人设备上的数据也会被替换。

你的权利和删除方法

数据只在你的设备、你自己的备份，以及开启家庭共享时房主的 iCloud 里，这些事你可以直接在 App 里做：

  • 查阅和复制：所有记录都能在 App 里看到，也可以导出成备份文件。
  • 更正：账户、持仓、现金、德州记录和成员都能直接修改。家务记录和净资产快照可以删除，家务删掉后可以重记。
  • 删除：删掉单条记录，或者删除 App，它在这台设备上的全部数据会一起删掉。iCloud 里的备份在 iPhone 的「设置」里管理和删除，电脑里的备份在电脑上管理。
  • 删除 iCloud 上的家庭数据：房主在「设置 › 家庭共享」点「关闭家庭共享」，App 会删除 iCloud 上的整份家庭数据，房主本机的数据保留，家人可以选择保留或清除自己设备上的数据。家人点「退出家庭」，会清除自己设备上的家庭数据，不影响其他人；你记过的内容会留在家庭里，想删掉的话，退出前先删除这些记录，或者请房主删除。

App 没有账号，不涉及注销。我们手里没有你的数据，没法替你查询、更正、删除或找回。对本政策有疑问，或者想知道 App 怎么处理某类信息，可以写信给我们。

你写信给我们时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和信件内容，只用来回复你，不用于营销，也不交给别人。邮箱由 Gmail 提供，邮件会经过 Google 的服务器。你可以随时要求我们删除往来邮件。

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026
Applies to the LifeTracker+ iPhone app ("LifeTracker+ 生活追踪" on the App Store) and this website

LifeTracker+ has no servers, and the developer collects no personal information. What you record is stored on your iPhone. With household sharing on, it is also stored in the owner's iCloud, and only the family members the owner invites can access it. When it looks up prices or exchange rates, the app sends ticker symbols and currency pairs to Yahoo Finance, which sees your IP address.

We collect no data

The app's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected". Household sharing data is stored in the owner's iCloud. Only the household can read and write it; neither the developer nor any third party can access it, so it is not collected data.

When the app goes online

LifeTracker+ has no servers of its own. It connects to Yahoo Finance only in these cases:

  • When you tap Refresh on the Assets tab, to look up prices and the exchange rates in use.
  • At launch, if exchange rates are 2 or more days old and you have records in other currencies.
  • When you use a currency for the first time, to look up its exchange rate.
  • When you change the base currency or tap Refresh Rates (刷新汇率) in Settings › Exchange Rates, or on the rates page opened from a missing-rate notice.

Requests contain only ticker symbols (such as AAPL or 0700.HK) and currency pairs (such as USD to SGD). They never include amounts, quantities, account names, members or any device identifier. As with any website, Yahoo sees your IP address and basic request information such as the time.

Household sharing

  • Household sharing is off by default. Until you tap Turn On Household Sharing (开启家庭共享) in Settings › Household Sharing (家庭共享), join an invitation or tap Connect to the Household in iCloud (连接 iCloud 上的家庭), the app uploads nothing to iCloud. Each of these requires being signed in to iCloud. This is the app's own iCloud sharing, separate from Apple's Family Sharing for purchases.
  • Once it is on, the records in the table above, plus the base currency, exchange rates, net-worth goal and the household's time zone, are uploaded to the owner's iCloud and count against the owner's iCloud storage. Module switches, privacy mode, gain/loss colors, rounding to thousands and "I am" stay on each device.
  • Only people the owner invites can join. The app offers no public link that anyone could use. Everyone who joins can view, add, edit and delete any record in the household. Family members read and write the copy in the owner's iCloud; the app keeps no separate copy in their iCloud.
  • Everyone in the household can see, under Household Sharing (家庭共享) in Settings, the iCloud names of other members and of people invited but not yet joined, or their iCloud email address or phone number if no name is available.
  • Apple transmits and stores the data under iCloud's rules. The app adds no encryption of its own. We have no servers and cannot see this data.
  • From 1.1.0, the app registers with the Apple Push Notification service at every launch, even with household sharing off, and never asks for notification permission. When a family member changes data, iCloud sends a silent push so the app syncs; no alert is shown. The app never sends the push token to us or any third party.
  • If household sharing is off and you open Settings, the app asks iCloud for your account status and whether iCloud holds a household you created or joined before. It only reads; it writes nothing.

Where data is stored and for how long

  • Data is kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone, where other apps cannot read it, and is protected by iOS Data Protection.
  • It stays on your device until you delete it. With household sharing on, records a family member deletes are removed from your device too.
  • With household sharing off, LifeTracker+ stores no data in any cloud. With it on, household data is also kept in the owner's iCloud until the owner turns household sharing off. Deleting the app does not delete household data in iCloud.
  • If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, the app's data is included in that backup and kept under Apple's rules in your own Apple Account or on your computer. We have no access to these backups.

Backup, export and import

  • In Settings, you can export all data, including data from modules you turned off, to a JSON backup file. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. You decide whom to send it to, for example by AirDrop.
  • Importing a backup replaces all records on this device. Before importing, the app shows the file name, export time and number of records, and acts only after you confirm. If something fails midway, your existing data is left unchanged.
  • While household sharing is on, only the owner can restore from a backup. The restored records sync to the whole household and replace the data on family members' devices.

Your rights and how to delete

Your data is only on your device, in your own backups and, with household sharing on, in the owner's iCloud. You can do all of this directly in the app:

  • Access and copy: see every record in the app, or export a backup file.
  • Correct: edit accounts, holdings, cash, 德州 records and members directly. Chore records and net-worth snapshots can be deleted, and a deleted chore can be logged again.
  • Delete: delete a single record, or delete the app to remove all of its data from this device. Manage or delete iCloud backups in Settings on your iPhone, and computer backups on your computer.
  • Delete household data in iCloud: the owner taps Turn Off Household Sharing (关闭家庭共享) in Settings › Household Sharing. The app deletes the whole household copy in iCloud, the owner's device keeps its data, and family members choose whether to keep or clear the data on their devices. A family member who taps Leave Household (退出家庭) clears the household data on their own device without affecting anyone else. Records you added stay in the household; delete them before leaving, or ask the owner to.

There is no account to close. We hold none of your data, so we cannot look it up, correct, delete or recover it for you. Email us if you have questions about this policy or want us to explain any processing.

If you email us, we receive your email address and message. We use them only to reply, never for marketing, and don't pass them on. Our mailbox is hosted by Gmail, so messages pass through Google's servers. You can ask us to delete our correspondence at any time.

Children
