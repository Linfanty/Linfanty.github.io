下面提到的「设置」，在总览页右上角打开。

成员和模块

怎么添加或修改成员？

在「设置 › 成员」里添加成员，改名字或颜色。只有一个成员时，记家务和德州不用选人。

归档和删除有什么区别？

还没有任何记录的成员可以直接删除。有记录的成员只能归档：记录都保留，成员从选择列表里隐藏，以后可以恢复。至少要留一个没归档的成员。

「共同」是什么？

不属于某一个人的账户，比如联名账户或家里的房子，可以设成「共同」。看「全部」时会算进去，也可以单独看「共同」。

怎么打开或关闭某个模块？

在「设置 › 模块」里开关。关掉只是隐藏，数据都保留。资产模块关闭期间，打开 App 时不会刷新汇率，也不会记录当天的净资产。保单每月缴费照常累计，重新打开后会补上这段时间的变动记录。

为什么我的 App 里没有德州？

德州默认关闭，在「设置 › 模块」里打开。它只记录线下打德州时每个人的盈亏、日期和地点，App 里不能打牌、下注或付款。

导入含德州记录的备份时，如果这台 iPhone 上从没开关过德州，导入后会自动打开。

价格和汇率

价格怎么更新？

在「资产」页点右上角的刷新按钮，App 会查询持仓的最新价格和用到的汇率。也可以在持仓里直接填现价。App 不会在后台自己刷新价格。

价格准吗？

行情数据来自 Yahoo Finance，可能有延迟，仅供参考。需要准确的数字时，以券商、银行或基金公司给你的为准。

刷新失败，或者有的代码查不到，怎么办？

先看网络是否正常。代码要按 Yahoo Finance 的写法填，比如港股 0700.HK、新加坡股票 D05.SI；A 股直接填 6 位代码。持仓的币种要和报价币种一致，比如美股选 USD，否则刷新时会提示币种不一致，不更新价格。行情服务查不到的持仓，手动填现价就行。

为什么提示缺少某个币种的汇率？

某个币种还没有汇率时，这部分金额暂时算不进总数。可以在「设置 › 汇率」里点「刷新汇率」，也可以手动填写。

汇率多久更新一次？

点刷新时更新。打开 App 时，如果汇率已有 2 天以上没更新，会自动刷新一次。第一次用到某个币种时，App 也会去查它的汇率。

可以换基准币种吗？

基准币种是所有金额汇总时用的币种，在「设置 › 汇率 › 基准币种」里换。换了以后，总数按新币种重新计算。以前的净资产记录里如果没有新币种的汇率，就用现在的汇率近似换算，图上标为估算。

备份和恢复

怎么备份？

在「设置」里点「导出全部数据 (JSON)」，再点出现的「分享备份文件」，存到「文件」App、电脑或其他稳妥的地方。备份包含全部记录、汇率和设置（不含模块开关），关掉的模块里的数据也在。文件不加密，请自己保管好。

怎么恢复？

在「设置」里点「从备份恢复…」，选中备份文件；也可以在「文件」App 或隔空投送里选择用 LifeTracker+ 打开备份文件。确认前会显示文件名、导出时间和记录数。

导入会替换这台 iPhone 上的全部记录，中途出错的话，原有数据不变。基准币种、净资产目标和汇率也换成备份里的。「我是」、私密模式这类显示设置，这台 iPhone 上设过的保留，没设过的用备份里的。

开着家庭共享时能恢复吗？

只有房主能从备份恢复，家人的设备上不能恢复。恢复的内容会同步给全家，家人设备上的数据也会被替换。

换手机怎么办？

开着家庭共享的话，在新手机上登录同一个 iCloud、装好 App，「设置 › 家庭共享」里会出现「连接 iCloud 上的家庭」，连接后数据就回来了。

没开家庭共享时，在旧手机导出备份，再到新手机导入。用 iCloud 云备份或电脑把整台 iPhone 恢复到新手机，数据也会跟过去。

家庭共享

怎么让家人一起记？

在「设置 › 家庭共享」点「开启家庭共享」，需要先登录 iCloud。等数据上传完，点「邀请家人」，用系统分享面板发给对方。

对方要在自己的 iPhone 上装好 LifeTracker+、登录 iCloud，再打开邀请，点「加入」。加入后对方 iPhone 上原有的记录会换成家庭的数据，之后全家改的内容会自动同步。

家人能改什么？

加入的人都能查看、添加、修改和删除任何记录。只有房主能邀请家人、管理共享和从备份恢复。

基准币种、汇率和净资产目标全家共用。模块开关、私密模式、涨跌颜色、金额按千取整和「我是」各自设置。

两个人同时改了同一条记录会怎样？

各自改的字段都保留；同一个字段两边都改了，一般用后改的那个，手动填的价格和汇率不会被随后的自动刷新盖掉。有人删了这条记录的话，同步后它会被删掉。

没网的时候能记吗？

能。改动先存在本机，联网后自动发送。「设置 › 家庭共享」里能看到上次同步的时间和还没发出去的条数，也可以点「立即同步」。

提示「iCloud 空间不足」怎么办？

家庭数据存在房主的 iCloud 里，占房主的空间。房主在 iPhone 的「设置」里清理 iCloud 空间或升级容量后，没发出去的改动会自动补发。

怎么退出或关闭家庭共享？

家人点「退出家庭」，自己 iPhone 上的家庭数据会被清除，不影响其他人。

房主点「关闭家庭共享」，iCloud 上的家庭数据会被删除，房主本机的数据保留。家人那边会显示「家庭共享已结束」，可以选「保留本机数据」或「清除本机数据」。

换了 iCloud 账号会怎样？

家庭共享会暂停，不再发送任何改动，本机和 iCloud 上的数据都保留。换回原来的账号后点「重试」，或者点「停止同步」改成只在本机使用，iCloud 上的家庭数据不会删除。

删除数据

怎么删除全部数据？

删除 App 会删掉它在这台 iPhone 上的全部数据，没法找回，建议先导出备份。iCloud 或电脑里已有的整机备份，在系统设置或电脑上管理。

删除 App 不会删除 iCloud 上的家庭数据。要删掉它，房主在「设置 › 家庭共享」点「关闭家庭共享」。

数据和隐私

开发者能看到我的数据吗？

看不到。我们没有服务器。记录存在你的 iPhone 上，会随你自己的 iCloud 云备份或电脑备份保存；开启家庭共享后，也存在房主的 iCloud 里，由 Apple 保管，只有家里的人能看到。

App 什么时候联网？

查价格和汇率时连接 Yahoo Finance，发出去的只有证券代码和货币对。App 每次启动会向 Apple 推送通知服务注册，打开「设置」时会向 iCloud 查询账号状态；开启家庭共享后，还会通过 iCloud 同步家庭数据。完整说明在隐私政策里。

Members and modules

How do I add or edit members?

In Settings › Members (成员) you can add members and change their names or colors. With only one member, logging chores and 德州 results doesn't ask who.

What is the difference between archiving and deleting?

A member with no records can be deleted. A member with records can only be archived: the records stay, the member is hidden from pickers and can be restored later. At least one member must stay active.

What does "Shared" (共同) mean?

Accounts that don't belong to one person, such as a joint account or the family home, can be set to Shared. They count toward "All" and can also be viewed on their own.

How do I turn a module on or off?

Use Settings › Modules (模块). Turning a module off only hides it; its data is kept. While Assets is off, opening the app does not refresh exchange rates or record the day's net worth. Insurance policies keep accruing their monthly amount, and the missed entries are added to their history once Assets is back on.

Why don't I see 德州 in my app?

德州 (a Texas Hold'em log) is off by default. Turn it on in Settings › Modules. It only records each member's result, the date and the venue of games played in person; there is no gameplay, betting or payment in the app.

When you import a backup that contains 德州 records and the module has never been switched on or off on this iPhone, it is turned on for you.

Prices and exchange rates

How are prices updated?

Tap the refresh button at the top right of Assets (资产). The app looks up the latest prices of your holdings and the exchange rates in use. You can also type a price into a holding. The app never refreshes prices in the background.

Are the prices accurate?

Market data comes from Yahoo Finance and may be delayed. It is for reference only. When you need exact figures, rely on your broker, bank or fund company.

A refresh failed, or a symbol can't be found. What now?

First check your connection. Enter symbols the way Yahoo Finance writes them, such as 0700.HK for Hong Kong or D05.SI for Singapore; mainland China A-shares can be entered as the 6-digit code. A holding's currency must match the currency it is quoted in, such as USD for US stocks; otherwise the refresh reports a currency mismatch and leaves the price unchanged. If the market data service doesn't know a holding, type its price by hand.

Why does the app say a currency's rate is missing?

Amounts in a currency without a rate are left out of totals for now. Tap Refresh Rates (刷新汇率) in Settings › Exchange Rates (汇率), or enter the rate by hand.

How often are exchange rates updated?

Whenever you refresh. When you open the app and the rates are 2 or more days old, they are refreshed once. The first time a currency is used, the app also looks up its rate.

Can I change the base currency?

The base currency is the one all totals are shown in. Change it in Settings › Exchange Rates › Base Currency (基准币种). Totals are then recalculated. Past net-worth records without a rate for the new currency are converted at today's rate and marked as estimates on the chart.

Backup and restore

How do I back up?

In Settings, tap Export All Data (导出全部数据 (JSON)), then Share Backup File (分享备份文件), and save it to the Files app, a computer or another place you trust. The backup contains all records, exchange rates and settings (except module switches), including data in modules that are turned off. The file is not encrypted, so keep it safe.

How do I restore?

In Settings, tap Restore from Backup (从备份恢复…) and choose the file, or open a backup file with LifeTracker+ from the Files app or AirDrop. Before you confirm, the app shows the file name, export time and number of records.

Importing replaces all records on this iPhone. If anything goes wrong midway, your existing data stays as it was. The base currency, net-worth goal and exchange rates also come from the backup. Display settings such as "I am" (我是) and privacy mode keep this iPhone's choice if you've set them here; otherwise they take the backup's.

Can I restore while household sharing is on?

Only the owner can restore from a backup; family members' devices can't. The restored records sync to the whole household and replace the data on family members' devices.

What if I switch phones?

With household sharing on, sign in to the same iCloud on the new phone and install the app. Settings › Household Sharing (家庭共享) then shows Connect to the Household in iCloud (连接 iCloud 上的家庭), and connecting brings your data back.

Without household sharing, export a backup on the old phone and import it on the new one. Restoring the whole iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup also brings the data along.

Household sharing

How can my family record together?

In Settings › Household Sharing (家庭共享), tap Turn On Household Sharing (开启家庭共享). You need to be signed in to iCloud. When the upload finishes, tap Invite Family (邀请家人) and send the invitation from the share sheet.

The other person installs LifeTracker+ on their own iPhone, signs in to iCloud, opens the invitation and taps Join (加入). The records already on their iPhone are replaced with the household's, and from then on everyone's changes sync automatically.

What can family members change?

Everyone who joins can view, add, edit and delete any record. Only the owner can invite people, manage sharing and restore from a backup.

The base currency, exchange rates and net-worth goal are shared by the household. Module switches, privacy mode, gain/loss colors, rounding to thousands and "I am" are set on each device.

What if two people edit the same record?

Fields that only one person changed are all kept. If both changed the same field, the later edit usually wins, and a price or rate typed by hand is not overwritten by an automatic refresh right after. If someone deleted the record, it is deleted after syncing.

Can I record while offline?

Yes. Changes are saved on your iPhone and sent once you're online. Settings › Household Sharing shows the last sync time and how many changes are still waiting, and you can tap Sync Now (立即同步).

The app says iCloud storage is full (iCloud 空间不足). What now?

Household data is stored in the owner's iCloud and uses the owner's storage. Once the owner frees up iCloud storage or upgrades it in Settings on their iPhone, waiting changes are sent automatically.

How do I leave or turn off household sharing?

A family member who taps Leave Household (退出家庭) has the household data cleared from their own iPhone. Nobody else is affected.

When the owner taps Turn Off Household Sharing (关闭家庭共享), the household data in iCloud is deleted and the owner's device keeps its data. Family members see Household Sharing Has Ended (家庭共享已结束) and can choose Keep Data on This iPhone (保留本机数据) or Clear Data on This iPhone (清除本机数据).

What happens if I switch iCloud accounts?

Household sharing pauses and sends no changes. Data on the iPhone and in iCloud is kept. Switch back to the original account and tap Retry (重试), or tap Stop Syncing (停止同步) to use the app on this iPhone only; the household data in iCloud is not deleted.

Deleting data

How do I delete all data?

Deleting the app removes all of its data from this iPhone. This can't be undone, so export a backup first if you may need it. Existing iCloud or computer backups of the whole iPhone are managed in Settings on the iPhone or on your computer.

Deleting the app does not delete household data in iCloud. To delete it, the owner taps Turn Off Household Sharing (关闭家庭共享) in Settings › Household Sharing.

Data and privacy

Can the developer see my data?

No. We have no servers. Your records are stored on your iPhone and saved with your own iCloud or computer backups. With household sharing on, they are also stored in the owner's iCloud, kept by Apple, and only your household can see them.

When does the app go online?

It looks up prices and exchange rates from Yahoo Finance, sending only ticker symbols and currency pairs. At every launch it registers with Apple's push notification service, and opening Settings checks your iCloud account status. With household sharing on, it also syncs household data through iCloud.
