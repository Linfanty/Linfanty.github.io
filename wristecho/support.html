开始使用

没有 Apple Watch 能用吗？

能。在「录音」页用 iPhone 录，锁屏也会继续；也可以导入已有的录音。

手表上的 App 在哪？

叫「回声」，需要 watchOS 11。没自动装上的话，在 iPhone 的 Watch App 里安装。

怎么加到表盘？

长按表盘 › 编辑 › 复杂功能，选「回声」。

能先看看效果吗？

在「设置 › 示例」里加载示例数据，看完可以一键清除。

录音

怎么知道正在录音？

手表上有红点和计时，iPhone 顶部有红点，系统也会显示麦克风提示。只有你点了开始才会录。

会自己停吗？

很久没人说话（默认 45 分钟）、手表电量低于 10%、iPhone 电量低于 5% 时，会自动停止并保存。

来电会打断录音吗？

会，这是系统规定。打断前的内容都会保存；接不上时手表会提醒你。

手表耗电多少？

Series 9 实测熄屏连录约 13 小时，用电约 74%，因机型和用法而异。

录音怎么传到 iPhone？

每 10 分钟一段，在 iPhone 旁边就自动传。iPhone 收到后，手表才删掉这一段。

占多少空间？

每小时约 14 MB。旧音频会按设置自动清理，文字一直保留。

转写和认人

支持哪些语言？

中文（普通话、台湾）、粤语、英语、日语、韩语、法语、德语、西班牙语、意大利语、葡萄牙语。

怎么认出我和家人？

在「设置 › 人物与声纹」里读 20 秒文字录声纹。录别人的声纹前，请先征得对方同意。

有错字、标错人怎么办？

长按句子可以编辑，点说话人标签可以改人。要紧的地方请回听原声。

日报

日报什么时候出来？

默认每晚 21:00 后自动整理，每周一出周报；也可以随时点「整理」。

日报是怎么写的？

支持 Apple 智能的 iPhone 用 Apple 智能；内存 6 GB 以上可以下载本地模型；其他情况用摘录整理，只挑原话。都在本机完成。中国大陆 App Store 版本只提供摘录整理。

本地模型从哪下载？

从 Hugging Face 下载开源模型 Qwen3，你确认后才开始，只下载、不上传。

试用和购买

怎么收费？

免费下载，7 天免费试用，到期不扣费。之后一次性解锁，不是订阅，支持家人共享。

试用结束不买会怎样？

录音照常保存，已有内容照常能看；新的转写和日报会暂停，解锁后自动补上。

怎么恢复购买、退款？

在购买页点「恢复购买」。退款请到 reportaproblem.apple.com。

数据和隐私

录音会上传吗？

不会发给开发者或任何第三方，转写和整理都在你的设备上。文字和声纹会随你自己的 iCloud 或电脑备份保存，音频默认不进备份。详见隐私政策。

怎么导出、删除？

「设置 › 存储」里导出全部文字；「设置」里可以删除全部数据。

能给 App 上锁吗？

在「设置 › 隐私」里打开面容 ID 锁定。

Getting started

Can I use it without Apple Watch?

Yes. Record on iPhone from the Recordings tab, even with the screen locked, or import recordings you already have.

Where is the watch app?

It's called WristEcho and needs watchOS 11. If it didn't install automatically, install it from the Watch app on your iPhone.

How do I add it to my watch face?

Touch and hold the watch face › Edit › Complications, then choose WristEcho.

Can I try it with sample data?

Load sample data in Settings › Sample, and clear it with one tap when you're done.

Recording

How do I know it's recording?

The watch shows a red dot and timer, the iPhone shows a red dot at the top, and the system shows its mic indicator. It only records after you tap start.

Does it stop by itself?

After a long silence (45 minutes by default), when the watch battery drops below 10%, or when the iPhone drops below 5%, it stops and saves.

Do calls interrupt recording?

Yes, the system requires it. Everything recorded before is saved, and the watch reminds you if it can't resume.

How much battery does it use?

In our overnight test, Apple Watch Series 9 recorded about 13 hours with the screen off and used about 74% of its battery. It varies by model and use.

How do recordings reach my iPhone?

In 10-minute segments, sent automatically when the iPhone is nearby. The watch deletes a segment only after the iPhone has it.

How much space does it take?

About 14 MB per hour. Old audio is cleaned up based on your settings; text is always kept.

Transcription and speakers

Which languages are supported?

Chinese (Mandarin, Taiwan), Cantonese, English, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese.

How does it recognize me and my family?

Read a short text for 20 seconds in Settings › People & Voiceprints. Ask other people for consent before enrolling their voice.

A word or a speaker is wrong?

Touch and hold a line to edit it, or tap the speaker label to change who said it. Check important details against the original audio.

Reports

When is the daily report ready?

By default it's written after 9 PM each evening, with a weekly report every Monday. You can also tap Summarize at any time.

How are reports written?

iPhones with Apple Intelligence use Apple Intelligence; iPhones with 6 GB of memory or more can download a local model; otherwise extracts pick your original sentences. All on the device. The China mainland App Store version offers extracts only.

Where does the local model come from?

The open-source Qwen3 model is downloaded from Hugging Face only after you confirm. Nothing is uploaded.

Trial and purchase

How much does it cost?

Free to download with a 7-day free trial, and nothing is charged when it ends. Then a one-time unlock, not a subscription, with Family Sharing.

What happens if I don't buy after the trial?

Recordings are still saved and existing content stays available. New transcription and reports pause, and catch up once you unlock.

How do I restore a purchase or get a refund?

Tap Restore Purchases on the purchase page. For refunds, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Data and privacy

Are my recordings uploaded?

They are never sent to us or any third party; transcription and reports happen on your devices. Transcripts and voiceprints are included in your own iCloud or computer backup, and audio is left out of backups by default. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I export or delete my data?

Export all text in Settings › Storage, and delete all data in Settings.

Can I lock the app?

Turn on Lock with Face ID in Settings › Privacy.
