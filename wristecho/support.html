开始使用

没有 Apple Watch 能用吗？

能。在「录音」页点「用 iPhone 录音」，锁屏后照样录。也可以在「录音」页右上角的「更多」里选「导入音频」，或者在「文件」等 App 的分享菜单里选「腕上回声」，导入已有的录音。

手表上的 App 在哪里？

手表上的 App 叫「回声」，需要 watchOS 11 或更新版本。装 iPhone 版时，如果手表打开了自动安装 App，会一起装好；没有的话，打开 iPhone 上的 Watch App，在「我的手表」页往下找到「回声」，点「安装」。

怎么把「回声」加到表盘？

在手表上长按表盘，点「编辑」，滑到复杂功能那一页，点一个位置，选「回声」。以后在表盘上点一下就开始录，录音时还会显示已录时长。

能先看看效果吗？

可以。在「设置 › 体验」里点「加载示例数据」，会加入几段虚构的对话，带说话人和标记，可以搜索、提问；开始 7 天免费试用以后，还能生成日报。看完点「清除示例数据」，不影响你自己的录音。

App 有英文或繁体中文界面吗？

有。App 界面支持简体中文、繁体中文和英文，跟随 iPhone 的系统语言；也可以在 iPhone 的「设置 › App › 腕上回声 › 语言」里单独选。

录音

怎么知道正在录音？

手表上，「回声」开在屏幕上时会显示红点和计时；加到表盘上的「回声」也会显示正在录音和已录时长。用 iPhone 录音时，App 每个页面的顶部都有红点、计时和停止按钮。系统也会显示麦克风正在使用的提示。录音只会在你自己开始之后进行（在 App 里点开始，或者用 Siri、快捷指令、操作按钮），不会自己开始。

能用 Siri、快捷指令或操作按钮录音吗？

能，用的是 iPhone 录音。腕上回声提供三个快捷指令：「用 iPhone 开始录音」「停止 iPhone 录音」「标记这一刻」。可以对 Siri 说「用腕上回声开始录音」，也可以在「快捷指令」App 里使用，或者在 iPhone 的「设置 › 操作按钮」里选「快捷指令」再选其中一个。开始录音会打开 App，第一次用之前需要先在 App 里同意录音须知。

怎么停止？会自己停吗？

手表上按住停止键一秒；iPhone 上点停止。下面几种情况会自动停止并保存：

  • 一直没人说话超过设定时间，默认 45 分钟，可以在「设置 › 录音」里改成 30 分钟到一个半小时，或者关掉
  • 手表电量低于 10%
  • iPhone 没在充电，电量低于 5%

手表上刚停下的几秒里，如果是误触，可以点「继续录」马上接上。

来电、闹钟、Siri 会打断录音吗？

会。通话、Siri 这类功能需要用麦克风时，系统会打断录音，这是 Apple 的规定。打断前录好的内容都会保存。

打断结束后，腕上回声会尝试自动接着录。手表系统不允许 App 在后台重新打开麦克风，所以有时接不上；这时手表会尽量发通知提醒你，回到「回声」点一下就能接着录。保险起见，抬腕时看一眼红点和计时还在不在。

手表录音耗电多少？

我们用 Apple Watch Series 9 做过一次过夜实测：熄屏连续录了约 13 小时，用掉约 74% 电量。不同型号、电池状况和使用习惯会有差别。想录一整天，出门前把手表充满电。

录音什么时候传到 iPhone？

录音每 10 分钟存成一段，存好就排队传给你配对的 iPhone。不在 iPhone 旁边时，录音先存在手表上，回到旁边再传；手表上的「立即同步」可以马上补传。iPhone 确认收到之后，手表才会删掉这一段。

会占多少空间？

音频按 32 kbps 压缩，每小时大约 14 MB。完全没人说话的段会立即删掉音频。「设置 › 存储」里的自动清理默认打开：只有陌生人和环境声的音频默认保留 14 天，有认识的人说话的默认保留 90 天（可以改成 30 天、1 年或一直保留）；还没录你自己的声纹时，认不出的声音也按认识的人的时间保留。标了 ⭐ 的段和还没转写的音频不会自动清理。iPhone 剩余空间不到 2 GB 时，会先清理只有陌生人和环境声的音频，还不够再清理 30 天以前的其他音频。清理只删音频，文字一直保留。

转写和认人

支持哪些语言？

中文（普通话）、中文（台湾）、粤语、英语（美国、英国）、日语、韩语、法语、德语、西班牙语、意大利语和葡萄牙语（巴西）。在「设置 › 转写」里选，默认跟随系统语言。

iPhone 12 及更新的机型用系统自带的离线识别，第一次用某种语言时要联网下载一次语言资源。更早的机型（iPhone 11 系列、iPhone SE 第二代）用 App 内置的模型，支持中文、粤语、英语、日语和韩语。

多久能看到文字？

录音传到 iPhone 后，App 开着时会马上排队转写。App 不在前台时，iPhone 接上电源后系统会找时间继续转，具体什么时候由 iOS 安排。还没开始试用或解锁时，新录音会先存着，开始后自动转写。

怎么让它认出我和家人？

在「设置 › 人物与声纹」里录你的声纹：找个安静的地方，用平时说话的音量读一段文字，大约 20 秒。家人和同事可以在本人在场、同意之后一起录；也可以先只添加名字，以后在录音里点说话人标签，选这个名字，标出这一段是谁说的。默认只标这一段；对方知道并同意被识别的话，可以在提示里选择记住这个声音，以后的录音会自动认出来，以前录音里还留着这个声音的地方也会标上。

说话人标错了怎么办？

在录音里点那句话前面的说话人标签，改成正确的人，或者选「不认识 / 环境声」。如果两个人的声纹太像，「人物与声纹」页会提示，建议在安静的地方重录其中一位。

转写有错字怎么办？

离得远、几个人同时说话、背景吵，都会让转写不准。长按那一句，选「编辑这句」就能改。要紧的地方请回听原声：点任意一句，就从这一句开始播放，一段放完会自动接着放下一段，速度可以选 1 倍、1.25 倍、1.5 倍或 2 倍。

AI 整理

日报什么时候出来？

默认每天 21:00 以后、当天的录音都转写完，就自动整理日报；时间可以在「设置 › AI 整理」里改成 18:00 到 23:00，也可以关掉。每周一会整理上一周的周报。你也可以随时在「记忆」页点「整理这一天」。允许通知的话，整理好会提醒你。

我的 iPhone 能用 AI 写日报吗？

「设置 › AI 整理」最上面会显示这台 iPhone 现在能用哪种方式：

  • Apple 智能：iPhone 15 Pro、iPhone 15 Pro Max，以及 iPhone 16 和之后的机型，打开 Apple 智能后，日报、周报和问答都由 Apple 智能在本机完成。设备语言要和 Siri 语言一致，并且是 Apple 智能支持的语言。在中国大陆购买的设备目前用不了 Apple 智能。
  • 本地模型：6 GB 及以上内存的 iPhone，可以在同一页下载开源模型（约 1 GB 到 2.3 GB），之后离线使用。
  • 摘录整理：每台 iPhone 都能用，从录音里挑出最有内容的原话，整理出待办和说好的事，不用生成模型。

如果以后在中国大陆 App Store 上架，那里下载的版本只提供摘录整理。

本地模型从哪里下载？安全吗？

从 Hugging Face（huggingface.co）下载公开的开源模型 Qwen3（Apache 2.0 许可），连不上时改用镜像站 hf-mirror.com。下载前会显示大小，你确认后才开始；文件会做完整性校验，中断了可以接着下。只下载，不上传任何内容。本地模型只在 App 打开时工作，整理一天大约要一两分钟。

AI 写得不对怎么办？

AI 会出错。日报的标题和摘要可以自己改，改过的内容不会被自动整理覆盖；也可以点「重新整理」。要紧的事请点时间回听原声。

试用和购买

怎么收费？

免费下载。在 App 里点「开始 7 天免费试用」，7 天内所有功能都能用，到期不会自动扣费。之后可以一次性解锁，买一次一直能用，不是订阅，支持家人共享。价格以 App Store 显示为准。

试用结束不买，会怎样？

录音照常传进来、照常保存；已有的文字、日报和周报照样能看、能搜、能导出、能删除。只是新录音的转写和新的 AI 整理会暂停。解锁后，攒下的录音会自动转写，最近 7 天的日报和上一周的周报会自动补上；更早的日子，可以在「记忆」页逐天点「整理这一天」。

换手机或重装后，怎么恢复购买？

用买的时候那个 Apple 账号登录 App Store，打开 App 的购买页面点「恢复购买」。试用记录也跟着 Apple 账号走，同一个账号只能试用一次。

家人能一起用吗？

一次性解锁支持家人共享。同一个家庭共享群组里的家人在自己的设备上安装腕上回声就能用，不用另外购买（购买人需要打开购买项目共享）。每个人的录音和数据都在自己的设备上，互相看不到。

怎么退款？

购买由 Apple 处理，请到 reportaproblem.apple.com 申请。

数据和隐私

我的录音会上传吗？

不会发给开发者或任何第三方。腕上回声没有服务器，录音、文字和声纹都在你的设备上处理和保存，开发者看不到。如果你打开了 iCloud 云备份或用电脑备份 iPhone，文字和声纹会随你自己的备份保存，见下一条。App 只在系统下载语音和语言资源、你选择下载本地模型、购买时联网，这些请求都不带你的内容。详见隐私政策。

换新 iPhone，数据怎么办？

音频默认不进 iCloud 备份，换机后不会恢复；文字、日报、待办和声纹会跟着 iPhone 的备份走，用备份恢复新 iPhone 时一起回来。搜索索引不进备份，恢复后 App 会从文字重新建立。想连音频一起备份，就在「设置 › 存储」里关掉「音频不进 iCloud 备份」。也可以随时用「导出全部文字」另存一份。

怎么导出？怎么删除？

「设置 › 存储 › 导出全部文字」会把所有转写按天整理成一个文本文件。单场录音和日报也能分享文字；单独一段可以在它的菜单里分享这段录音或这段文字。

「设置 › 删除全部数据」会删除这台 iPhone 上的录音、文字、声纹、日报、待办和搜索记录，设置会保留。还在手表上、没传过来的录音之后仍会传到 iPhone。

别人拿到我的 iPhone，能看到录音吗？

可以在「设置 › 隐私」里打开应用锁。打开后，每次打开 App 或离开超过 1 分钟再回来，都要用面容 ID、触控 ID 或锁屏密码验证，多任务界面里也看不到内容。

Getting started

Can I use it without an Apple Watch?

Yes. On the Recordings tab, tap Record with iPhone; recording continues when the screen locks. You can also import existing recordings: choose Import Audio from the More menu at the top right of the Recordings tab, or pick WristEcho in the share sheet of Files or another app.

Where is the watch app?

WristEcho on Apple Watch needs watchOS 11 or later. If automatic app install is on, it installs with the iPhone app. Otherwise open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll down on My Watch to WristEcho and tap Install.

How do I add it to my watch face?

Touch and hold the watch face, tap Edit, swipe to the complications screen, tap a slot and choose WristEcho. From then on, one tap on the face starts recording, and the complication shows how long you've been recording.

Can I try it out first?

Yes. In Settings, tap Load Sample Data. It adds a few fictional conversations with speakers and stars that you can search and ask about; once you start the 7-day free trial, you can also see them in reports. Tap Clear Sample Data when you're done; your own recordings are not affected.

Is the app available in English or Traditional Chinese?

Yes. The app comes in English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese and follows your iPhone's language. You can also pick a language just for WristEcho in iPhone Settings › Apps › WristEcho › Language.

Recording

How do I know it's recording?

On Apple Watch, the app shows a red dot and a timer while it's on screen, and the WristEcho complication on your watch face shows that it's recording and for how long. When you record on iPhone, every screen of the app shows a bar at the top with a red dot, a timer and a Stop button. The system also shows its microphone-in-use indicator. Recording only happens after you start it yourself (in the app, or with Siri, Shortcuts or the Action button); it never starts on its own.

Can I record with Siri, Shortcuts or the Action button?

Yes, for recording on iPhone. WristEcho offers three shortcuts: start iPhone recording, stop iPhone recording and mark a moment. Say "Start recording with WristEcho" to Siri, use them in the Shortcuts app, or set the Action button to Shortcut in iPhone Settings › Action Button and pick one. Starting a recording opens the app, and you need to accept the recording notice in the app before first use.

How do I stop? Does it stop by itself?

On the watch, hold the stop button for one second; on iPhone, tap Stop. It also stops and saves by itself when:

  • nobody has spoken for a set time, 45 minutes by default; change it from 30 minutes to an hour and a half, or turn it off, in Settings › Recording
  • the watch battery drops below 10%
  • the iPhone battery drops below 5% while not charging

If you stopped the watch by mistake, tap Resume within a few seconds to carry on.

Will calls, alarms or Siri interrupt recording?

Yes. When a call or Siri needs the microphone, the system interrupts the recording; that is how Apple's platforms work. Everything recorded up to that point is saved.

When the interruption ends, WristEcho tries to resume automatically. watchOS does not let apps reopen the microphone from the background, so sometimes it can't; the watch then tries to notify you, and one tap in the app gets you going again. To be safe, glance at the red dot and timer when you raise your wrist.

How much battery does watch recording use?

In one overnight test, an Apple Watch Series 9 recorded for about 13 hours with the screen off and used about 74% of its battery. Results vary with model, battery health and how you use the watch. For a full day, start with a fully charged watch.

When do recordings reach my iPhone?

Audio is saved in 10-minute segments, and each one is queued for your paired iPhone as soon as it's saved. Away from your iPhone, segments wait on the watch and go across once you're back; Sync Now on the watch sends them right away. The watch deletes a segment only after the iPhone confirms it arrived.

How much storage does it take?

Audio is compressed at 32 kbps, about 14 MB per hour. Segments with no speech have their audio deleted right away. Automatic cleanup in Settings › Storage is on by default: audio with only strangers or background sound is kept 14 days, and audio with people you know 90 days (or 30 days, 1 year or forever). Until you record your own voiceprint, unrecognized speech follows the people-you-know period. Starred segments and audio not yet transcribed are never cleaned up automatically. With less than 2 GB free, the app first removes audio with only strangers or background sound, then other audio older than 30 days. Cleanup removes audio only; text always stays.

Transcription and speakers

Which languages are supported?

Chinese (Mandarin), Chinese (Taiwan), Cantonese, English (US and UK), Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese (Brazil). Choose one in Settings › Transcription; by default it follows your system language.

iPhone 12 and later use Apple's built-in offline recognition, which downloads its language files once the first time you use a language. Earlier models (iPhone 11 series, iPhone SE 2nd generation) use the model built into the app, which covers Chinese, Cantonese, English, Japanese and Korean.

How soon do I see the text?

Once a recording reaches your iPhone, it is queued for transcription right away while the app is open. When the app isn't in front, iOS continues in the background while the iPhone is charging, at a time iOS chooses. Before you start the trial or unlock, new recordings are stored and get transcribed once you do.

How does it learn to recognize me and my family?

Record your voiceprint in Settings › People & Voiceprints: in a quiet place, read the short text at your normal volume for about 20 seconds. Family and colleagues can record theirs when they're present and agree. You can also add just a name and later tap a speaker label in a recording to pick that name, which labels that segment. If the person knows and agrees to be recognized, you can choose to remember their voice when asked. Later recordings will recognize them, and earlier recordings that still hold their voice are labeled too.

A line is labeled with the wrong person. What now?

Tap the speaker label in front of the line and choose the right person, or choose unknown / background sound. If two voiceprints are too similar, the People & Voiceprints page tells you and suggests re-recording one of them somewhere quiet.

The transcript has mistakes.

Distance, people talking over each other and background noise all reduce accuracy. Touch and hold a line and choose Edit Line to fix it. For anything important, listen to the original: tap any line to play from there, at 1×, 1.25×, 1.5× or 2×. Playback carries on into the next segment.

AI summaries

When does the daily report appear?

By default, after 9 PM once the day's recordings are transcribed. Change the time from 6 PM to 11 PM, or turn it off, in Settings › AI. Every Monday you get a report on the week before. You can also write up a day at any time from the Memory tab. With notifications on, you hear when a report is ready.

Can my iPhone write reports with AI?

The top of Settings › AI shows what this iPhone can use:

  • Apple Intelligence: on iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, and iPhone 16 or later with Apple Intelligence turned on, reports and answers are written by Apple Intelligence on the device. Your device and Siri language must match and be supported by Apple Intelligence. Devices bought in China mainland can't currently use Apple Intelligence.
  • Local model: iPhones with 6 GB of memory or more can download an open-source model (about 1 GB to 2.3 GB) on the same page; it then works offline.
  • Extract mode: works on every iPhone. It picks the most meaningful sentences from your recordings and lists to-dos and promises, with no generative model.

If WristEcho is later offered on the China mainland App Store, copies downloaded there will offer extract mode only.

Where does the local model come from? Is it safe?

It is the open-source Qwen3 model (Apache 2.0), downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), with the mirror hf-mirror.com as a fallback. You see the size and confirm before it starts; files are checked for integrity, and an interrupted download picks up where it left off. Nothing is uploaded. The local model only works while the app is open, and writing up a day takes a minute or two.

The AI got something wrong.

AI makes mistakes. You can edit a report's title and summary, and your edits are kept when reports refresh, or you can rebuild the report. For anything important, tap the time to hear the original audio.

Trial and purchase

How much does it cost?

WristEcho is free to download. Tap Start 7-Day Free Trial in the app to use every feature for 7 days; nothing is charged when it ends. After that, a one-time unlock keeps everything working for good. It is not a subscription and supports Family Sharing. The price is shown in the App Store.

What happens if I don't buy after the trial?

Recordings keep arriving and stay safe, and your transcripts, daily and weekly reports stay readable, searchable, exportable and deletable. Only transcription of new recordings and new AI reports pause. Once you unlock, saved recordings are transcribed automatically, and daily reports for the last 7 days and last week's weekly report catch up. For older days, tap Generate Daily Report on that day in Memory.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought with, open the purchase screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases. The trial is tied to your Apple Account too, so each account can use it once.

Can my family use it?

The one-time unlock supports Family Sharing. Members of your Family Sharing group can install WristEcho on their own devices without buying it again, as long as the purchaser has purchase sharing turned on. Each person's recordings and data are kept on their own devices, and no one sees anyone else's.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Data and privacy

Are my recordings uploaded?

Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party. WristEcho has no servers; recordings, transcripts and voiceprints are processed and kept on your devices, and the developer cannot see them. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, transcripts and voiceprints are included in your own backup (see the next question). The app goes online only when iOS fetches speech and language files, when you choose to download a local model, and for purchases. None of these requests carries your content. See the privacy policy.

What happens to my data on a new iPhone?

Audio is left out of iCloud Backup by default, so it won't come back on a new phone. Transcripts, reports, to-dos and voiceprints are part of your iPhone backup and return when you restore from it. The search index isn't backed up; the app rebuilds it from your transcripts after a restore. To include audio, turn off Keep Audio Out of iCloud Backup in Settings › Storage. You can also save a copy at any time with Export All Text.

How do I export or delete my data?

Settings › Storage › Export All Text puts all transcripts, by day, into one text file. You can also share the text of a single recording or report, and the audio or text of a single segment from its menu.

Settings › Delete All Data removes recordings, transcripts, voiceprints, reports, to-dos and search history from this iPhone and keeps your settings. Segments still on the watch will reach the iPhone later.

Can someone with my iPhone see my recordings?

Turn on App Lock in Settings › Privacy. The app then asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode every time you open it or return after more than a minute, and hides its content in the app switcher.
