手表录音，iPhone 整理

录音、转写、认人、搜索和日报，都在你自己的设备上完成。

手表一点就录

在「回声」里点开始，或者把它加到表盘上，抬腕一点就录。「回声」开在屏幕上时有红点和计时，表盘组件和系统的麦克风提示也会显示在录音，放下手腕也不会停。

认得出谁在说话

读 20 秒文字录下你的声纹，你说的话会标成「我」。家人、同事在场并同意后也能录，或者直接在录音里给说话人起个名字。

搜得到，问得出

按关键词搜全部录音；记不清原话，就用一句话问。回答附上出处，点一下回到当时的录音。

日报、周报和待办

每天晚上整理当天：忙了什么、时间线、待办和说好的事；每周一出周报。待办点一下就能加进「提醒事项」。

没有手表也能用

直接用 iPhone 录，锁屏后照样录；用 Siri、快捷指令或操作按钮也能开始。也可以导入手机里已有的录音文件。

数据由你自己保管

没有服务器，不用注册，也不用填 API Key。文字、声纹、日报和待办会随你自己的 iCloud 或电脑备份保存，音频默认不进备份，还可以用面容 ID 锁住 App。

从手腕到日报

录音每 10 分钟存成一段，一段一段传到 iPhone，转完就能搜。

  1. 开始录音

    手表或 iPhone 上点一下。很久没人说话或电量太低时，会自动停止并保存。

  2. 传到 iPhone

    录好的段自动传到你自己的 iPhone，收到后手表上的那一段才删除。

  3. 转成文字

    iPhone 在本机转写，标出说话人，建好搜索索引。充电时也会在后台继续转。

  4. 整理日报

    默认每晚 21:00 以后整理当天，每周一整理上一周。允许通知的话，整理好会提醒你。

AI 整理，看你的 iPhone

没有 AI 也能用：录音、转写、认人和搜索都不依赖它。

Apple 智能

支持 Apple 智能的 iPhone

iPhone 15 Pro、iPhone 15 Pro Max，以及 iPhone 16 和之后的机型，打开 Apple 智能后，日报、周报和问答都由 Apple 智能在本机完成。

本地模型

下载一个开源模型

6 GB 及以上内存的 iPhone，可以在设置里下载开源模型（约 1 GB 到 2.3 GB），下好以后离线使用。

摘录整理

每台 iPhone 都能用

从录音里挑出最有内容的原话，整理出待办和说好的事，不用任何生成模型。

Apple 智能能不能用，取决于设备、语言和所在地区，中国大陆目前用不了。AI 整理可能有错，要紧的事请回听原声核对。

没有服务器，不发给开发者或第三方

腕上回声不收集任何数据，没有账号，也没有广告和统计工具。开发者看不到你的录音、文字和声纹，数据只在你的设备和你自己的备份里。设置里随时可以导出全部文字，或者删除全部数据。

Record on your wrist, sorted on your iPhone

Recording, transcription, speaker recognition, search and reports all happen on your own devices.

One tap on Apple Watch

Tap Start in the app, or add it to your watch face and start with one tap. The app shows a red dot and timer while it's on screen, and the complication and system mic indicator show it too. Recording keeps going with your wrist down.

Knows who's talking

Read a short text for 20 seconds and your lines are labeled "Me". Family and colleagues can enroll when they're present and agree, or just name a speaker in a transcript.

Search it, ask it

Search every recording by keyword, or ask in your own words. Answers cite the original lines; tap one to hear that moment again.

Daily and weekly reports

Every evening: what the day was about, a timeline, to-dos and promises made. Every Monday, a weekly report. Send to-dos to Apple Reminders with one tap.

No watch? No problem

Record with your iPhone, even with the screen locked, or start with Siri, Shortcuts or the Action button. You can also import audio files you already have.

Your data stays yours

No servers, no sign-up, no API keys. Transcripts, voiceprints, reports and to-dos go into your own iCloud or computer backup, audio is left out of backups by default, and you can lock the app with Face ID.

From your wrist to a daily report

Audio is saved in 10-minute segments, sent to your iPhone one by one, and searchable once transcribed.

  1. Record

    Tap once on the watch or iPhone. It stops and saves by itself after a long silence or when the battery runs low.

  2. Send to iPhone

    Segments go to your own iPhone automatically. The watch deletes a segment only after the iPhone has it.

  3. Transcribe

    Your iPhone transcribes on the device, labels speakers and builds the search index, and keeps going in the background while charging.

  4. Write it up

    After 9 PM by default it writes up the day, and every Monday the week before. With notifications on, you hear when it's ready.

AI summaries depend on your iPhone

Recording, transcription, speaker recognition and search work without any AI.

Apple Intelligence

iPhones with Apple Intelligence

On iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, and iPhone 16 or later with Apple Intelligence turned on, reports and answers are written by Apple Intelligence on the device.

Local model

Download an open-source model

iPhones with 6 GB of memory or more can download an open-source model (about 1 GB to 2.3 GB) in Settings. After that it runs offline.

Extracts

Works on every iPhone

Picks the most meaningful sentences from your recordings and lists to-dos and promises, with no generative model.

Apple Intelligence availability depends on device, language and region, and it is currently not available in China mainland. AI can be wrong, so check important details against the original audio.

No servers. Nothing sent to us.

WristEcho collects no data. There is no account, no ads and no analytics, and the developer never sees your recordings, transcripts or voiceprints. Your data stays on your devices and in your own backups. Export all your text or delete everything in Settings at any time.
